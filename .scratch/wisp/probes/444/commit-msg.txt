winsec: seven race-window instruments stop calling a starved route a dead guardrail (444)

Ticket 441 drew the line and one instrument used it; these seven still counted two
different things inside one `if` - the racer's ARRIVAL (did the window exist at all)
and the route's LANDING (did a PrivateFile call stand inside it). Under load either
zero is legitimate: the route is a paced loop (2ms per attempt) against a window a
few microseconds wide, and a pinned handle can make the racer's own rename fail
outright ("Access is denied."), so often neither side arrives. Every such zero
printed RED, i.e. the instrument shouted "the guardrail is gone" exactly when it knew
the least. Measured before the change: 6/6 RED at a shrunk denominator on the
Windows ancestor-open instrument, 5/5 on the removal one, 2/2 on the seam's hop arm,
3/3 on each POSIX instrument.

Each of the seven now has two carriers instead of one:

  - the racer-side zero stays RED, byte for byte. If the racer proves no cycle the
    fixture machinery is gone and no future run could test anything - that must fail;
  - the route-side zero prints a named denial carrying that run's numbers:
    ABSTAIN(444:<shape>:<why>) attempts=N samples=S ... and ends by saying what does
    carry the row instead;
  - a NEW un-raced control reaches the window deterministically - pin the parent,
    flip the ancestor, perform the one act through the pin, then read back the
    foreign witness and the caller's own leaf - and reds if the act left the pinned
    object or the foreign name was touched. The control is the threshold now; the
    raced sample is evidence.

Seven mutations on an out-of-repo tree say the controls have teeth: oNoFollowPlatform=0,
Openat->Open(path), Unlinkat->os.Remove(join), Root.OpenFile->os.OpenFile(join),
Root.Remove->os.Remove(join) twice (once at the seam), and the refusal-path removal
call deleted from winsec.go. Each went RED naming the leg verbatim, each was restored
to the same SHA256 and went green again. The last one prints both
"the removal seam was reached 0 times in 400 attempts" and the new abstention line -
a denial does not swallow a red.

Nothing was deleted or relaxed. Six red arms are kept verbatim, six are split into
(kept red + new denial), seven deterministic control reds are added, and one early
`return` is gone: the 342-unix "not a landing reading" branch used to return before
the damage assertions, so on the run where it had least to say it also said nothing
about damage. Those assertions now run.

Production bytes changed: 0. No thresholds, goldens or contracts touched, no test
hidden behind a build tag, no t.Skip.

Evidence, with the verbatim sentence each instrument used to print and the reading it
prints now: docs/evidence/s1/444-instrument-abstention.md. Logs for every number:
.scratch/wisp/probes/444/logs/.
