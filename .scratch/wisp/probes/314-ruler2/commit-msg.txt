ticket 314: the shape ruler now judges the leaf it actually names, so the five assembled shapes, the three other roots and the package-level sinks the audit walked through are no longer silent.

D1 was spelling: the matcher read one literal token, so "wisp-33r1"+"-panel-profile" was invisible. Leaves are constant-folded first (+ chains, strings.Join over a literal slice, a Join with a literal suffix), and the fold refuses a leaf that is only part constant, because a constant prefix plus a pid is a per-run name and not this defect. D2 adds the roots that are really on this machine: os.Getenv(TEMP) and TMP, os.UserHomeDir including its two-result form, and a drive-letter absolute literal. A lone absolute literal is a path rather than a join, so it is left to the sink rule; the first run proved why, because the ruler convicted two bare sample paths in its own control table. That was over-reach and it is fixed in the rule, not in the exemption table. D3 walks package-level declarations through both rules and seeds package-level fixed joins into every function body, so a var declared once and handed to a sink elsewhere is caught. D4 adds the three missing resident controls (newPanelControl, the DataPath field, the DataPath: key) and they are load-bearing: breaking any one arm now fails its own control, which the audit measured as all-green. D5 prints hits, exempted and actually-red plus the exemption self-check count, and says red whenever any of them is non-zero, so a stale exemption can no longer fail this test while the summary reads "0 red".

A1b and A1c are still out and named as such in the header: both need a value to cross a function boundary, which is a different instrument. The two exemptions are untouched. Readings: docs/evidence/s1/314-shape-ruler.md section 7, .scratch/wisp/probes/314-ruler2/.
