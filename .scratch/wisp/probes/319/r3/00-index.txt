`GetForegroundWindow() == hwnd` is NOT satisfiable on today's tree by the routed
executor, and closing it would mean touching mgr.Show - explicitly frozen for
this leg ("不改 RequestShow/mgr.Show"), so this leg stopped there per 未定义即停
and left the reasons where a reader cannot miss them: the pin's doc comment and
its "RAISE HALF OF AC#2, READ NOT ASSERTED" log line, this index, and the leg's
receipt. What the probes isolate, each from a clean state: the activation's own
mgr.Show leaves the foreground on the prior window (A, and A2 one second later);
ShowWindow(SW_SHOW) alone does not activate an already-visible window (C), while
SetForegroundWindow alone does (B). Reading of mgr.Show: it calls
SetForegroundWindow only when the window was NOT already shown
(panel_host_windows.go, Show's tail), which is exactly the shape the cold and
hidden cases need and the visible case lacks. Candidate fix, for the
orchestrator: drop that guard for the re-show branch (one line), or add a named
raise hop - either is a contract-shaped decision this leg may not take.
